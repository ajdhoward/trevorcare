# Family guide — who uses what, in two taps

Everything below is reachable from one sidebar. Dark mode is the default;
the light theme is one toggle away. Every entry is audited where it matters.

## Alex (you — admin, family lead)

- **My dashboard** answers first: both parents' today-picture, responsibilities
  due, new alerts, latest from WhatsApp + Mum's home.
- **My tasks / Calendar** — your duties for Dad and Mum with .ics phone
  sync; shopping list fills itself from visit sheets + WhatsApp.
- **LPA & availability** — tick the 20 statutory items; Pat links her
  calendar (no login needed) and gets suggested mutually-free days.
- **Family circle** — Life360: who's inside Dad's home radius, batteries,
  check-ins.
- **Share with advisers** — generate the social worker's WhatsApp link (14-day default).
- **AI review bridge** — download the brief, let any AI review it, paste the
  reply back, apply items with one tap.

## Pat (co-LPA, wants zero hassle)

1. Accept your invite (Access-protected in production).
2. Open **LPA & availability** → paste your calendar's .ics link once.
3. The portal suggests days you're both free → tap to accept/decline
   proposals. Confirmed joint slots land on the family calendar.

## Extended family (Contact A, Contact B — tiered by consent)

- You see what the consent matrix says (lifestyle / activities), never more.
- Contribute to **Dad's WhatsApp group** — shopping needs and updates you
  post flow into the portal automatically and feed his care flows.

## The social worker (no account — token only)

- Receives a **time-boxed read-only brief** link: both parents' current
  picture, 30-day flag themes, known record issues, "what the family would
  like help with". Expires by itself; Alex can revoke instantly.

## Carers (agency, minimum data)

- Shift view only: today's picture, medication, conditions & mitigation,
  schedule, **visit task sheets** — including the "items for Dad's list"
  field (spot the bleach → add it → the family's shopping list updates).

## Dad ("My Day" tablet)

- `/kiosk`: giant clock, who's coming, tap-boards for dinner/drink/outfit/
  activity, family photo wall, calming anchors, one huge call button.
- Choices appear in the family portal ("My Day choice received") and in the
  audit trail. Nothing he taps can break anything.

## Mum — represented by the family)

- **Mum's care** tab: contact log with follow-ups, 1–5 well-being tracker,
  escalation ladder (home → manager → the care-home group → the council → CQC → MASH),
  email/phone templates, WhatsApp-group plan for the home.
- Her well-being is a dashboard card and a DCPI-adjacent signal — the
  council-facing check `chk-mum-home` alerts if contact goes quiet.

## The two golden rules

1. **Mum and Dad first**: every layout decision serves their well-being and
   your control — features that serve neither are in Workspace & tools.
2. **If it isn't audited, it didn't happen**: corrections, shares, sends and
   AI feedback all land in the tamper-evident trail automatically.
